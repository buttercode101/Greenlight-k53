# GreenLight K53 — store listing draft

Status: copy prepared from current functionality; no AAB has been built or uploaded, and this document is not a published listing.

App name: GreenLight K53 Learner Practice

Short description: Practise SA K53 signs, rules and controls with short drills and mock tests.

Full description:

Prepare for your South African learner's licence with a clear study path.

Start with six questions to identify a useful next lesson. Study illustrated road signs, selected road rules and controls for learner Codes 1, 2 and 3. Choose short topic sets, review mistakes and try a learning mock or timed 64-question practice simulation.

GreenLight includes:
- Illustrated signs, explanations and read-aloud through supported browser voices.
- Topic practice in 10-question, 20-question or full available sets.
- A shared mistake queue with short review rounds.
- Code 2 road-decision scenarios inside Practise.
- A practice-readiness index alongside question coverage and first-attempt evidence.
- Offline study after a successful initial download.
- Local progress and downloadable backups without requiring an account.

The current web version is free and has no ads. Read-aloud voice availability depends on the device. Drive scenarios currently cover Code 2 only; teaching is English only.

GreenLight is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with the South African Government, RTMC, NaTIS or a DLTC. Questions are original selected practice, not the official question bank or a complete syllabus. Independent subject review remains pending. Practice scores are not a guarantee of passing. Use the current official manuals and confirm arrangements with your DLTC.
