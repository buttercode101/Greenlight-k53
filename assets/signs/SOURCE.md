# GreenLight K53 sign illustrations

The files in this directory are self-contained SVG study illustrations generated by
`tools/generate-sign-assets.mjs`. They replace a mixed set of third-party SVGs and
failed Wikimedia HTML downloads that could render as broken images.

The illustrations consistently use the visual families taught for Southern African
road signs: red regulatory signs, blue mandatory/information signs, yellow warning
triangles, and pedestrian signal boards. They are intentionally labelled as study
illustrations, not reproductions of an official publication. The question content
and current local rules must always be confirmed against the applicable SADC Road
Traffic Signs Manual and the learner's local DLTC.

`tools/verify-assets.mjs` checks that every referenced sign is an SVG, contains a
viewBox, has a title, and that no legacy PNG/HTML placeholder remains.
