K53 study guide: prepare for your learner's licence test

Use this quick guide alongside the free interactive practice test. It covers the three areas learners need to revise: road signs, rules of the road and vehicle controls.

Start a free timed K53 mockStudy road signs
Study-smart tip: Do not rely on one overall score. Practise every section until you can consistently meet its required mark. Confirm the current requirements, booking process and fees with your local DLTC before your appointment.

1. Road signs: learn the shape first

Red circleProhibition or restriction: something is not allowed.
Blue circleMandatory instruction: you must follow it.
Yellow triangleWarning: slow down, scan ahead and prepare.
Red octagonSTOP: make a complete stop, then proceed only when safe.
Inverted triangleYIELD: give way where necessary; do not assume priority.
Rectangle / boardInformation, direction or a service such as parking.

Then identify the symbol inside the sign. Study each sign with its meaning and the action it requires; use the road-sign library and flashcards to practise recognition.

2. Rules of the road: the recurring test themes

  • Observation before movement: check mirrors and blind spot, signal, then manoeuvre only when it is safe.
  • Right of way: at an uncontrolled crossing, give way to traffic approaching from your right. Obey any signs, signals or officer instructions first.
  • Pedestrians: approach crossings carefully and yield as required. Never overtake at or near a pedestrian crossing.
  • Speed: follow the posted limit and reduce speed for conditions, visibility, hazards and road users.
  • Emergency vehicles: make space safely and give way to an emergency vehicle using its warning devices.

3. Vehicle controls: be able to explain the basics

Know the purpose of the steering wheel, pedals, gears, handbrake, mirrors, indicators, lights, wipers and demisters. The test can ask what a control does and when it should be used. Revise focused scenarios in the vehicle-controls session.

4. Build confidence with a realistic mock test

  1. Study signs, rules and controls in separate short sessions.
  2. Use drills to revisit the category that feels least certain.
  3. Take the 64-question mock test with the timer on.
  4. Review every explanation, especially incorrect answers, and retake weak sections.

Important note

GreenLight K53 is a free study aid, not an official government service. Rules, fees, booking requirements and local licensing procedures can change. Check with your Provincial Department of Transport or local Driving Licence Testing Centre for authoritative, current information.
