# Phase 3 launch brief

## Positioning
Don't memorize a fixed test. Learn the rules, practise them, find weak areas, and improve before your learner's test.

## ICP
Primary user: SA learner-driver candidate with a booked/intended test in the next 1-6 weeks. Buyer: same user/parent if premium is later validated. Trigger: test date, failed attempt, uncertainty about the newer randomized/computerised experience. Trust barrier: whether material matches authoritative K53/NaTIS guidance.

## Activation
Complete one learning/practice session and one scored assessment section.

## North star
Learners who complete a practice cycle and then return to improve a weak area.

## Monetization
Keep core learning free while content review is still incomplete. Test willingness to pay later for convenience/advanced practice, not access to essential rules. Current market anchors include ~R49.99/week and ~R149.99/month subscriptions; GreenLight should earn trust before copying them.

## Experiments
1. Search-targeted K53 study guide -> exact lesson/test. Success: lesson-to-assessment activation. 14 days.
2. Driving-school pilot ask: free link for learners, request aggregate feedback only. Success: 2 schools willing to share it. 21 days.
3. Share-score/progress behavior observation. Success: organic referred sessions that activate. 30 days.

## Launch copy
**One line:** Free K53 practice that shows what you understand, what you keep getting wrong, and what to study next.

**Community-safe post:** If you're preparing for the newer randomized K53 learner test, I built GreenLight around understanding the rules rather than memorising one fixed paper. It's independent, free, and points learners back to official material. I want feedback from people writing soon: which questions feel unclear, and which weak-area explanations actually help?
